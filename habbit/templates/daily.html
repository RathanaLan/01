High-Leverage Productivity Playbook

Battle-tested mental models to execute your day effortlessly

🍅 Pomodoro Technique

Work with intense single-minded focus for 25 or 50 minutes, followed by an immediate 5 to 10 minute screen-free break. Prevents mental fatigue and eliminates task procrastination.

🎯 Deep Work (Cal Newport)

Schedule your hardest intellectual challenges when willpower is peaked (8:00–10:00 AM). Treat this time like a doctor's surgery: zero phone, zero Slack, zero background tabs.

🧱 Time-Blocking & Batching

Group shallow tasks (emails, Slack replies, invoice checks) into dedicated 45-minute communication windows instead of reacting to notification pings every 3 minutes.

⚡ 2-Minute Rule & Habit Stacks

Stack new habits onto established triggers: "After I brew morning tea, I will journal 3 priorities." If a small task takes less than 2 minutes, execute it immediately.

Evening Sleep Architecture

The 10-3-2-1-0 Rule for Deep Sleep
  • 10 hours before bed: No more caffeine (after 12:00 PM).
  • 3 hours before bed: No large heavy meals or alcohol (finished by 7:00 PM).
  • 2 hours before bed: Cease stressful work tasks and heavy planning.
  • 1 hour before bed: Screen curfew. Dim lights; read physical book or journal.
  • 0: The number of times you hit snooze tomorrow morning.
