1. Scope & Governance

This Privacy Policy outlines how the DNKH Digital Transformation Portal and its affiliated applications, including the 5M1E Production Tracker & Quality Suite, manage employee access credentials, smart factory telemetry, process change points, and manufacturing audit logs.

Data stewardship is directed under Rathana Lan, Digital Transformation Supervisor, focusing on transparency, high-integrity data custody, and strict adherence to industrial standards including ISO 9001 and IATF 16949.

2. Information Collected

We capture operational and diagnostic telemetry necessary for manufacturing excellence:

  • Operator & Authentication Data: Corporate email address, full name, skill tier certification, and OAuth provider token claims (Microsoft 365 Entra ID, Google Workspace, Facebook SSO).
  • Production 5M1E Telemetry: Machine cycle times, operating pressures, temperatures, spindle speeds, material batch numbers, SOP codes, and calibration metrics.
  • Digital Audit Signatures: Operator timestamps, revision history, and countermeasure documentation.

3. Security, Storage & Encryption

All production records and user accounts are secured using:

  • Cloud PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS): Hosted on Supabase infrastructure ensuring tenant isolation.
  • Cryptography: Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) for all APIs; AES-256 for all stored database records.
  • Credential Protection: Passwords are cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) managed by Supabase Auth; plaintext secrets are never stored.

4. User Rights & Data Portability

Operators and supervisors retain full rights to:

  • Inspect their audit records and assigned shift parameters.
  • Rectify erroneous logs via authorized supervisor workflows.
  • Export complete quality logs in structured JSON format for quality control reviews.
