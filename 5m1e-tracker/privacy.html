2. Information Collected Across 5M1E Dimensions

The Application is designed for comprehensive shop-floor traceability and root-cause anomaly detection. When operators, technicians, and quality inspectors record or edit production events, the following data is logged:

  • 1. MAN Operator & Personnel Data:
    Operator Full Name, Employee Identifier, Skill Tier (e.g. Tier 1 to Tier 4), Active Certification Status, and qualification logs.
  • 2. MACHINE Equipment & Tooling Telemetry:
    Station ID, Machine Running State (Running, Idle, Breakdown, Setup), Operating Temperature (°C), Hydraulic/Pneumatic Pressure (bar), Spindle RPM, and Tool Wear/Life cycles.
  • 3. MATERIAL Raw Material & Lot Traceability:
    Component Part Number, Supplier Batch/Lot Number, Certified Supplier Identity, and Certificate of Analysis (CoA) verification records.
  • 4. METHOD Process Specifications & Procedures:
    Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Reference Number, Machine Recipe Identifier, Standard Cycle Time (s), and Actual Cycle Time (s).
  • 5. MEASUREMENT Quality Metrology & Tolerance:
    Inspection Gauge/Tool ID, Calibration Due Date, Critical Quality Feature, Nominal Dimension, Lower Specification Limit (LSL), Upper Specification Limit (USL), and Observed Measurement value.
  • 6. ENVIRONMENT Ambient Facility Telemetry:
    Cleanroom Ambient Temperature (°C), Relative Humidity (RH%), Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Grounding verification, and Cleanroom Class standard (e.g. ISO 7).

Additionally, each event record captures administrative metadata: a unique record ID (`LOG-YYYY-XXX`), UTC timestamp, line assignment, shift designation, work order, digital audit signature, and countermeasure resolution history.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis for Processing

We process manufacturing quality and operational data strictly under legitimate industrial interests and contractual necessity:

  • Root-Cause Analysis: Enabling automated Ishikawa (Fishbone) diagram mapping to isolate manufacturing defects.
  • Quality Assurance & Regulatory Compliance: Providing complete traceability for automotive, electronics, and precision manufacturing audits.
  • Equipment & Process Health: Detecting out-of-spec deviations in temperature, pressure, cycle times, and tool life.
  • Audit Trail Integrity: Maintaining non-repudiable logs of who recorded, modified, or countermeasured each production anomaly.

No Commercial Exploitation: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker shop-floor data or personnel records to any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Data Security, Storage & Encryption

The Application utilizes enterprise-grade defense-in-depth security standards:

PostgreSQL & Row-Level Security

Data is hosted on managed PostgreSQL databases powered by Supabase with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies enforcing role-based read and write isolation.

TLS 1.3 & AES-256 Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 cryptographic suites. Data at rest is encrypted using FIPS 140-2 validated AES-256 bit block ciphers.

Production records are maintained for the duration mandated by the relevant manufacturing customer agreement or statutory product liability retention schedules (typically 7 to 15 years for safety-critical components).

5. Identity Providers & Single Sign-On (SSO)

The Application supports industry standard OAuth 2.0 and OpenID Connect (OIDC) identity providers:

  • Microsoft 365 (Entra ID / Azure AD): Validates corporate organizational accounts, extracting authorized email, full name, and tenant security claims.
  • Google Identity Services: Provides corporate Gmail / Google Workspace authentication tokens.
  • Facebook / Meta Login: Used for authenticated shop-floor access testing in designated production environments.
  • Supabase Auth: Manages token issuance, session refresh, and encrypted JWT tokens.

Passwords entered during standard email authentication are salted and hashed with bcrypt on Supabase infrastructure; plaintext passwords are never transmitted or stored by the Application.

6. Operator Rights & Data Portability

In accordance with international privacy frameworks (GDPR Articles 15–22, CCPA/CPRA), operators and authorized personnel retain the right to:

  • Access & Inspection: Review all personal records, operator certifications, and shift logs associated with their credentials.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate operator records via the authorized record editing functionality or plant supervisor review.
  • Data Portability: Export production logs and change-point parameters in structured JSON format via the built-in Backup & Restore facility.
  • Right to Erasure / Anonymization: Where manufacturing quality regulations permit, operators may request that their personal identity on historic logs be pseudonymized.

7. LocalStorage, Cookies & Offline Cache

To maintain uninterrupted responsiveness on factory floors with intermittent WiFi or cellular signal, the Application uses browser localStorage for:

  • Display preferences (Compact vs Normal density, Dark mode theme state).
  • Custom production floor line and shift definitions.
  • Temporary local cache of active production logs ensuring zero data loss during network disruptions.

The Application does not deploy tracking pixels, third-party advertising cookies, or behavioral profiling trackers.
