Legacy Factory Reality

Work moved. Information did not.

Manufacturing lines operated with physical precision, but administrative processes were held back by paper checksheets, fragmented Excel files, and communication silos.

Paper Forms & Excel Dependency

Shift teams recorded daily job tasks onto physical checksheets, then manually transcribed numbers into local Excel files, creating recurring typographical errors and discrepancies.

Manual Approval Handoffs

Paper requests sat on desks awaiting supervisor stamps. If a leader was on the shopfloor or in meetings, critical job requests were stalled for hours.

Low Operational Visibility

Management had zero real-time view into task bottlenecks or open jobs. Performance reports were compiled after the shift ended, rendering intervention purely reactive.

Frontline Pain

Repeated manual handoffs, verbal chasing of signatures, and duplicated administrative effort consumed up to 30 minutes of operator time every cycle.

Process Risk

Critical tasks could be delayed, missed entirely, recorded inconsistently, or lost in paper piles, risking line takt synchronization.

Business Impact

Prolonged lead times, administrative waste across 120+ factory staff, and inability for management to make proactive, data-driven decisions.
