## 🎯 Course Purpose & Philosophy
The **QA Network (Quality Assurance Network)** is a proven Japanese industrial methodology designed to:
1. **Build Quality at the Process:** Eradicate the misconception that quality can be inspected into a product at final QC. Quality must be designed into every individual manufacturing step.
2. **Apply the 1-30-300-3000 Prevention Rule:** Eliminate defects at Level D (In-Process) and Level C (Final Check) before they can cascade to Level B (OEM Vehicle Plant) or Level A (Vehicle End User).
3. **Map Defect Pathways:** Uncover how non-conformities are generated, transferred across stations, detected, or allowed to escape.
4. **Enforce 5 Gen Shop-Floor Realism:** Ground all quality audits in *Genchi Genbutsu (Go & See)*, observing actual parts (*Genbutsu*), real facts (*Genjitsu*), engineering principles (*Genri*), and standard rules (*Gensoku*).
