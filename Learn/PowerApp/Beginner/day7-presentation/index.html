Why do real business rules require && and ||?

Answer: Because workplace reality involves interdependent checks—like requiring both pressure AND coolant for machine safety.

From Day 3: Data Types

Why must we define whether data is Text, Number, or Boolean?

Answer: Because computers store them differently; mathematical comparisons and logic require strict typing (e.g. Value()).

From Days 1-2: Input vs Action

What separates an Input from an Action in an app?

Answer: An Input is information entered by a person; an Action is the work performed by the app in response.

Core Mental Model

Plain Language: What is an "App Opportunity"?

Understanding when a workplace friction point warrants an application before touching software.

❌ What is NOT an App Opportunity

False Leads & Wasteful App Projects

Problems that do not need custom software and are better handled through communication or existing tools.

One-time tasks (e.g. voting on the annual holiday party venue).
Static information (e.g. posting the weekly cafeteria lunch menu).
Management policy issues (e.g. workers arriving late to shift meetings).
✔ What IS an App Opportunity

Prime Candidates for Power Apps

Repetitive operational hand-offs where lost data, delayed approvals, or lack of visibility causes business loss.

✔ Repetitive Hand-off: Person A requests; Person B acts.
✔ Mobile Data Capture: Floor inspections, audits, work tickets.
✔ Automated Logic & SLA: Urgent issues alert managers automatically.
Key Insight: "Automating a broken process just gives you an automated mess." Always diagnose and fix the human hand-off before building the digital tool!

Stakeholder Architecture

Deconstructing the Three Essential Workplace User Roles

Every operational application coordinates three distinct human roles, each requiring a tailored screen experience.

👨‍🔧

1. The Requester (Creator)

Line Operator / Factory Floor

Needs: Ultra-fast input (< 60s), large touch buttons for work gloves, auto-captured time and machine defaults.
🧰

2. The Worker (Actor)

Maintenance Tech / Toolmaker

Needs: Mobile push notifications, clear list of open tickets sorted by urgency, 1-tap "Accept" and "Complete" buttons.
📊

3. The Supervisor (Viewer)

Plant Manager / Shift Lead

Needs: High-level visual KPI dashboard, overdue SLA warnings, mean time to repair, exportable historical records.
Feasibility Evaluation

The 4-Point App Feasibility Litmus Test

A systematic rubric to decide whether to build a custom Power App or use a simpler alternative tool.

The 4 Power Apps Litmus Tests
📅 1. High Frequency: Occurs daily or weekly, generating recurring operational friction.
📱 2. Mobility & Floor Capture: Used away from desks (tablets, barcode scanners, cameras).
⚙ 3. Logic & Formulas: Needs dynamic validation, required fields, and conditional warnings.
🔁 4. Multi-Stage Hand-off: Person A logs ➔ Person B acts ➔ Person C monitors status.
When to Use Other Microsoft 365 Tools
📧 Simple Email / Teams: One-off question or casual notification between two colleagues.
📋 Microsoft Forms: Simple survey or poll with zero logic, zero editing, and no hand-offs.
📊 Excel / SharePoint List: Standalone data tracking managed by a single person at a desk.
🛠 Physical Work: Adjusting the room temperature or fixing broken equipment directly.
Logic Translation

Extracting Concrete Business Rules from Human Speech

Bridging informal workplace desires into structured condition-verdict-action logic gates.

Human Speech ➞ Structured Rule

"Handle critical leaks immediately!"

Vague emotion translated into an exact compound condition and automated action.

If(Urgency = "Critical" && Type = "Leak", SoundBeacon(), Queue())
✔ Evaluates both factors before triggering plant-wide alarm.
Human Speech ➞ UI Interlock

"Don't let them submit half-empty tickets!"

Translates into button DisplayMode validation ensuring complete data capture.

If(!IsBlank(ddMachine.Value) && !IsBlank(txtNotes.Text), Edit, Disabled)
⚠ Prevents corrupted database records before submission!

Mental Model Corrections

4 Common Beginner Misconceptions & Trainer Fixes

Correcting plausible but dangerous wrong mental models before they lead to abandoned app projects.

❌ Wrong Mental Model #1

"Power Apps is for any cool idea I have in my head."

Trainer Correction: Software must serve an operational purpose. If there is no real pain, no clear user, and no lost money, nobody will use the app.

❌ Wrong Mental Model #2

"The more input fields on the screen, the better the app."

Trainer Correction: Floor operators wearing gloves will abandon forms with 30 text boxes. Capture the absolute minimum data needed to take action.

❌ Wrong Mental Model #3

"I should start by drawing the buttons and screens first."

Trainer Correction: Design always flows forward: Problem ➔ Process ➔ Data ➔ Screen. Designing screens first guarantees rework.

❌ Wrong Mental Model #4

"Digitizing a broken paper process will fix the bottleneck."

Trainer Correction: If workers don't know who is responsible on paper, an app will just produce digital confusion. Fix the human workflow first.

Exemplar 5-Part Opportunity Blueprint

  • 1. Problem: Whiteboard requests 200m away cause 40 mins idle time per mold change ($210,000/yr loss).
  • 2. Result: Instant mobile alert to die room; pre-stage molds 15 mins prior; changeover ≤ 8 mins.
  • 3. Roles: Operator (Logs run completion), Toolmaker (Installs mold), Scheduler (Tracks downtime).
